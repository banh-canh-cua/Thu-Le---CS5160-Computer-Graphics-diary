## Design
My idea is to build an interactive 3D scenery, inspired by retro arcade games.

The scene shows a landscape with multiple objects and layers that has two modes (day and night):
- Sky with sun/ moon, clouds, and stars
- Mountain ranges
- Pine tree forest
